# Long-form editorial criteria

Evaluate the drafts against the request. A careful editor may preserve a passage verbatim. Do not reward length reduction or number of edits on their own. Report ranking for each case, allow ties, and prioritise factual fidelity, scope, structure, and author's voice before stylistic preference.

## Technical cases zh1 and en1

- Track the distinction between all workspaces and the smaller cache-enabled log sample. Track jobs included in reporting windows, completed-job p95, and file delivery versus job completion. Compare the table exactly.
- Preserve evidence and status, including the concurrent retry change, unmatched workloads where stated, open or excluded tickets, and actions planned or unscheduled. Preserve scheduled versus API scope and each next-decision prerequisite. Treat adding a cause or resolving an unknown as a substantive error.
- Preserve all required rerun conditions, the unknown-status path, who is responsible, and the limited purpose of a command, escalation message, or deduplication rule. Preserve link targets, headings, quotes, and command. Judge whether a busy reader can find and correctly apply the rule in one pass.
- Check whether each paragraph moves forward while retaining scope reminders where they prevent a false conclusion. An explicit repetition may be operationally useful.

## Essay cases zh2 and en2

- Preserve quotation words, paragraph structure, time ordering, known relationships, and narrative facts. Leave open what the narrator leaves open.
- Follow images and references across the entire draft. Assess the recurrence of objects and understated closing. Preserve dialect, spelling, and intentionally terse or repeated lines where useful.
- Cut visibly generic sentences without replacing them with a lesson. If a nested sentence is repaired, track who acted and what they acted on.

## Required output format

For each case, write a separate heading `## zh1` etc. The first line under the heading is the ranking in form `A = B > C > D`. Then give 2–4 brief points citing actual before/after phrases and any material defects. A variant may rank below another for a minor tradeoff. Separate that judgement from factual or structural defects. Avoid assuming why an editor made a choice.
